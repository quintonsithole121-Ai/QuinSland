# Marketing Strategy for Going Viral 🚀

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Week 1-2)

### Build Hype
- [ ] Create teaser videos on TikTok/YouTube Shorts
- [ ] Build Discord community (target: 5,000 members)
- [ ] Set up email newsletter
- [ ] Create Reddit communities
- [ ] Partner with 10 micro-influencers (10K-100K followers)

### Influencer Outreach
- Offer free premium access
- Give early access 1 week before launch
- Create affiliate links for referral tracking
- Set performance bonuses for top promoters

## Phase 2: Launch (Week 3-4)

### Day 1 Launch Blitz
- [ ] Post simultaneously across all social media
- [ ] Go live on Twitch with top gaming creators
- [ ] Launch YouTube launch trailer (target: 1M views)
- [ ] Send press releases to gaming media
- [ ] Offer launch day bonuses ($10 free credits)

### Launch Incentives
- First 10,000 players get $10 bonus currency
- Referral bonus doubled for first week ($10 per referral)
- Limited cosmetics for early players

## Phase 3: Growth (Month 2-3)

### Content Strategy
- [ ] Daily TikTok clips (funny moments, wins)
- [ ] Weekly YouTube tutorials
- [ ] Discord events and contests
- [ ] Twitch streamers playing 24/7 (stream farm)
- [ ] Reddit AMAs (Ask Me Anything)

### Viral Mechanics
- Built-in social sharing for replays
- "Challenge your friends" feature
- Seasonal leaderboards with prizes
- Meme-friendly game moments

### Paid Advertising
- TikTok ads targeting gamers (start: $5K/day)
- YouTube gaming channels ($3K/day)
- Facebook/Instagram ads ($2K/day)
- Reddit gaming subreddits ($1K/day)

## Phase 4: Scale (Month 4+)

### Expansion
- [ ] International marketing (Spanish, French, etc.)
- [ ] Console versions (PlayStation, Xbox)
- [ ] Web version launch
- [ ] Esports tournament circuit
- [ ] Branded merchandise

### Community Growth
- Esports teams/sponsorships
- Gaming convention presence
- Community tournaments
- Professional player roster

## Social Media Content Ideas

### TikTok (Post 3x daily)
- Funny fail compilations
- Clutch moments
- Tips & tricks
- Meme videos
- "POV: You just installed QuinSland"
- Reaction videos to user gameplay

### YouTube (Upload 2x weekly)
- Full gameplay videos
- Tutorial series
- Top plays compilation
- Livestreams
- Behind-the-scenes development

### Instagram/Twitter (Post 4x daily)
- Game highlights
- Community achievements
- Announcements
- Engagement polls
- Memes

### Discord (Daily engagement)
- Daily challenges
- Community events
- Moderation contests
- Voice chat tournaments
- Exclusive sneak peeks

## Influencer Program

### Tiers

**Nano (1K-10K followers)**
- $100 per video
- Revenue share: 10%

**Micro (10K-100K followers)**
- $500 per video
- Revenue share: 15%
- Exclusive cosmetics

**Macro (100K-1M followers)**
- $2,000 per video
- Revenue share: 20%
- Early access to new features

**Mega (1M+ followers)**
- Negotiable
- Revenue share: 25%+
- Creative control, branded cosmetics

## Viral Loop Mechanics

### Built into Game:
1. **Share Replay** - Players share epic moments
2. **Challenge Friends** - "Beat my score" links
3. **Refer & Earn** - Both get bonuses
4. **Season Pass Preview** - Show non-players what's coming
5. **Leaderboard Bragging** - Automatic share buttons
6. **Achievement Sharing** - Unlock badges, share on social

## Target Audience

- Age: 13-35
- Demographics: Male 60%, Female 40%
- Interests: Gaming, esports, streaming
- Platforms: TikTok, YouTube, Discord, Twitch
- Games they play: Fortnite, Among Us, Roblox, Valorant

## Budget Allocation

- Influencer partnerships: $15,000 (30%)
- Paid ads: $20,000 (40%)

- Content creation: $5,000 (10%)

## Success Metrics to Track

- Downloads per day
- Daily Active Users (DAU)
- Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- User retention (Day 1, Day 7, Day 30)
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Viral coefficient
- Social media growth rate
- Influencer ROI

## Crisis Management

**If Launch Flops:**
- Pivot to niche gaming communities
- Release major content updates weekly
- Run special events to re-engage players
- Increase referral bonuses
- Partner with larger streamers

**If Technical Issues:**
- Daily updates and communication
- Compensation to affected players
- Prioritize fixes over new features
- Transparency in post-mortems

## Long-term Growth

Year 1: 5M+ downloads, $2-5M revenue
Year 2: 20M+ downloads, $10-20M revenue
Year 3: 50M+ downloads, $50M+ revenue
